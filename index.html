About

Over three years into GIS and remote sensing, with a Master's in Remote Sensing & GIS, I work at the intersection of satellite data, hydrological modelling and disaster risk reduction — with a particular focus on Glacial Lake Outburst Flood (GLOF) assessment.

I've led geospatial analyses for flood modelling, damage assessment and inventory management at the Aga Khan Agency for Habitat (AKAH), and before that worked across cadastral mapping, land record systems and telecom network planning at GIS-focused firms in Pakistan. Alongside client work, I provide scientific writing support and mentor students in GIS and remote sensing through my GIS Automator channel.

What I Do

Six ways I put geospatial data to work

Programming and software are tools in service of a bigger question: where is the risk, who does it affect, and what should be done about it.

01

GIS & Spatial Analysis

Geoprocessing, cartography and geodatabase management in ArcGIS Pro, ArcGIS Online and QGIS.

02

Remote Sensing

Satellite image analysis and classification using Google Earth Engine, ENVI and Erdas Imagine.

03

Hazard & Risk Assessment

GLOF, flood, landslide and reservoir-sedimentation risk assessment for mountain communities.

04

Hydrological Modelling

Flood, dam-break and crop-water-requirement modelling with FLOW-2D, HEC-RAS, SWAT and CROPWAT.

05

Cartography & Map Design

Layout maps and geospatial products designed for clear communication with non-technical audiences.

06

Web GIS

Interactive, browser-based maps built with Leaflet and Mapbox for public-facing spatial data.
